Class: WirehairDecoderRaw

WirehairDecoderRaw()

Encapsulates the Wirehair decoding functionality for raw packets (no headers). Use this class to decode a series of raw data packets back into the original message.

Constructor

new WirehairDecoderRaw()

Creates an instance of WirehairDecoderRaw. Only usable after calling await initWirehairModule(). Consider using WirehairDecoderRaw.create() instead.
